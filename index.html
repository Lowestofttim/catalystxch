What it does

Everything a market maker needs

CATalyst handles the mechanics so you can focus on the strategy.

📊
Live Bid/Ask Ladder
Deploys a multi-tier ladder of buy and sell offers around the current mid-price, sized proportionally by distance. Inner offers are largest, outer offers are smallest.
⚡
Auto-Requote
Monitors the market continuously. When price moves beyond your threshold, stale offers are cancelled and replaced — keeping you always on the right side of the spread.
🧠
Smart Settings
One click generates a complete capital plan. Smart Settings reads your wallet balance, live market data, and volatility, then calculates the optimal offer sizes, tier counts, and spread width.
🔭
Mempool Fill Detection
Fills are detected the moment they appear in the mempool — not after block confirmation. This means faster requotes and tighter spread capture.
🪙
Coin Prep
Chia offers each lock a specific coin. CATalyst pre-splits coins to exact sizes ahead of time so new offers are always ready to go without waiting for wallet operations.
🛡️
Risk Controls
Hard price bounds, position limits, inventory skew, circuit breakers, and configurable reserve amounts keep your capital protected at every market condition.
📈
Market Intelligence
Pulls live data from TibetSwap, Dexie, and Spacescan — AMM price, order book depth, pool size, and competitor spread — to inform pricing decisions in real time.
🖥️
Native Desktop App
Runs as a proper Windows desktop application with a system tray icon, native notifications, and a persistent window — no browser tab to close by accident.
🔗
Sage Wallet Integration
Connects directly to your local Sage wallet via RPC. Your keys never leave your machine — CATalyst talks to Sage, Sage talks to the blockchain.

The loop

How it works

CATalyst runs a continuous loop. Here's what happens every cycle.

Step 01
Price Check
CATalyst reads the current mid-price from TibetSwap's AMM and cross-references it with the Dexie order book. Competitor spreads are factored in to keep your pricing competitive.
Step 02
Ladder Management
Active offers are compared against the new price. Any that have drifted outside your requote threshold are cancelled and replaced with fresh offers at the correct prices and sizes.
Step 03
Fill Handling
Filled offers are detected via the mempool. The bot logs each fill, updates PnL, adjusts inventory skew if needed, and queues a replacement offer immediately.
Step 04
Coin Replenishment
Spare pre-split coins are consumed as offers are placed. When stock runs low, the topup worker automatically splits new coins in the background — the ladder keeps running uninterrupted.

Installation

Setup Guide

From a fresh machine to a running market maker in under ten minutes.

1
Install Sage Wallet
Download and install Sage Wallet (v0.12.10 or later). Once installed, open Sage and let it sync. Then go to Settings → Advanced and enable RPC. This is what allows CATalyst to communicate with your wallet.
2
Add your CAT token to Sage
In Sage, use the token manager to add the CAT token you want to market-make. Make sure your wallet holds a balance of both that token and some XCH — you'll need both to place offers on both sides of the spread.
3
Download and run CATalyst
Download the Windows installer from the Download section above and run it. CATalyst will install and open automatically. The first time it launches it will check that Sage is running and walk you through connecting your wallet.
4
Select your trading pair
Once connected, choose the CAT token you want to trade from the pair selector at the top of the dashboard. CATalyst will load the current market price from TibetSwap and show you a snapshot of the token's recent volume and price range.
5
Run Smart Settings
Click Smart Settings in the configuration panel. CATalyst will analyse your wallet balance, the current market conditions, and token volatility, then generate a complete set of recommended settings — offer sizes, tier counts, spread width, reserves, and coin prep targets. Review them and click Apply.
6
Run Coin Prep, then Start
Before the bot can place offers it needs coins pre-split to the right sizes. Click Run Coin Prep and wait for it to complete — this takes a few minutes the first time. Once done, hit Start Bot. The ladder goes live immediately.
💡 Tip: leave CATalyst running in the background. It sits in the system tray and keeps the ladder active while you work.
